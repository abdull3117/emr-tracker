Add a sale

Select the Sales Officer and Dealer from the list, or choose “+ Add new…” to enter one that isn't there yet. Product accepts free text. Quantity is optional but needed for the quantity projection on the Officer Performance tab.

Import from CSV, Excel or ZIP

Upload a CSV or Excel (.xlsx/.xls) file exported from Excel, myBillBook or Tally to add many rows at once — or a ZIP bundling several such files together (e.g. a folder of daily exports), and every spreadsheet inside is imported at once. myBillBook Group Summary (party ledger) and Tally Sales Register (voucher day-book) exports are both recognised automatically, across every sheet in the workbook — a Tally Sales Register import fills in Company, Invoice No., Product, Quantity and Value per invoice. For a plain flat file, the first sheet is used, and column headers can be in any order/case — recognised names: Date, Officer, Dealer, Company, Area, Product, Quantity, Unit, Invoice No, Value, Collected, Notes. Only Date, Dealer and Value are required; everything else is optional and left blank if missing.

Make PDF & Send to WhatsApp

Print opens your computer’s printer dialog directly for whatever is currently filtered above — if it doesn’t respond, open this page’s own link in a full browser tab (rather than viewing it inside the chat) and try Print there. Save as PDF always works — it downloads a PDF file you can open and print from any device. Make PDF & Send to WhatsApp builds that same PDF and shares it straight through your device’s share sheet when it supports it (pick WhatsApp there) — that only works on devices where the browser supports sharing a file this way (most phones; not most desktop browsers). Where it isn’t supported, WhatsApp opens with a text summary as before and the PDF is downloaded alongside it, so attach that downloaded file in WhatsApp yourself. All three follow the Officer / Product / search filters above.

Record a receipt

Log a collection against a dealer's outstanding balance directly if you don't have a fresh Tally export to hand.

Import from CSV, Excel or ZIP

Upload a CSV or Excel (.xlsx/.xls) export from Tally (a Receipt Register / Day Book filtered to Receipt vouchers) to add many rows at once — or a ZIP bundling several such exports together, and every spreadsheet inside is imported at once. A Tally Receipt Register is recognised automatically, the same way the Sales tab recognises a Sales Register — each voucher becomes one receipt row with dealer, reference and amount filled in. The Sales Officer isn't reliably present in a Tally receipt voucher, so it's filled in automatically from that dealer's most common officer in the Sales log where one can be found — double-check it after importing and correct any row where it guessed wrong. For a plain flat file, the first sheet is used, and column headers can be in any order/case — recognised names: Date, Officer, Dealer, Company, Amount, Reference, Notes. Only Date, Dealer and Amount are required.

Payments by vendor

Opening balance is what was owed to a vendor before this app started tracking purchases & payments — click into the field (in the All time view) to set or edit it, it saves automatically. Closing balance = Opening balance + Purchased − Paid, and shows what's currently owed. Switch the financial year above to review a single year: Purchased/Paid then cover just that year, and "Balance b/f" rolls in every earlier year automatically so Closing balance still shows the true running balance at the end of that year.

Import from Batch Book

Upload a CSV or Excel (.xlsx/.xls) export from Batch Book's Reports or Job Work tab to bring in production output and job-work revenue — or a ZIP bundling several such exports together, and every spreadsheet inside is imported at once. For a workbook with multiple sheets, the first sheet is used. Recognised column names (any order/case): Date, Product, Company, Output MT, Rate per MT, Revenue, Cost, Profit. Only Date and Product are required — leave Company blank for regular (non job-work) production.

Batch Book dispatch reconciliation

Compares what Batch Book's Stock ledger shows as dispatched each day against what's logged as sold in the Sales log that same day (matched on date, and on company when Batch Book has one set). Read-only — Claude keeps this synced from Batch Book automatically once a day; ask Claude to sync sooner if you need the very latest numbers.

Product name mapping

Batch Book and the Sales log sometimes name the same product differently (for example Batch Book's “No.18” may be the Sales log's “STD Mix : 18”). Map any Batch Book product that doesn't already match a Sales log product name so the reconciliation above can pair them up correctly.

The sales officer roster and their Area/Territory assignment now live in Settings — add or edit officers there and they'll appear in every report below automatically.

Sales officer — value & quantity projection

Projected next month = a straight-line trend fit through each officer's monthly totals so far (at least 2 months of data). Quantity projection only appears once quantity has actually been logged for that officer — most historical rows below don't have it yet. Use the dropdown to jump straight to one officer's report.

Add salary & incentive drawn

Record what each Sales Officer draws in salary and incentive for a given month. This feeds the performance & revenue report below, which weighs what an officer earns against the revenue and collections they bring in.

Performance & revenue report

Each officer's sales revenue and collections (from the Receipts tab) weighed against what they drew in salary and incentive — net contribution is revenue minus total pay. Officers are tiered so it's easy to see who to recognise and who needs attention.

New reconciliation entry

Record the balance each department holds for a creditor or debtor, and attach their supporting statement (PDF, image, or CSV attach directly; Excel workbooks are not supported for direct attachment — if the upload fails, export that sheet as PDF or CSV instead and the balance is still saved). Tag a Sales Officer so a confirmation statement can be generated for them below. Any discrepancy is flagged automatically and sorted to the top — high priority.

Document attachments are not available in this view right now — balances can still be recorded and compared; notify Claude if the attachment option needs to be enabled.

Creditor / debtor balance reconciliation

Discrepancies are sorted to the top and highlighted — these require priority follow-up between the Accounts and Verification departments.

Dealer statement & ledger

Pick a dealer to see their complete ledger — every sale (debit) and receipt (credit) in date order with a running balance — and generate a ready-to-send statement for follow-up or marketing outreach. Opening balance is what the dealer owed before this app started tracking sales & receipts; the field is editable in the All time view and saves automatically. Switch the financial year above to review a single year — Sales/Received then cover just that year, and "Balance b/f" rolls in every earlier year automatically.

Sales officer statement & ledger

Pick a sales officer to see their complete ledger — every sale they made (debit) and every receipt they collected (credit) in date order with a running outstanding total — plus every dealer that officer alone has sold to, for marketing aids and a report of their area. Only this officer's own dealers show here, even if another officer shares the same Area/Territory. Area comes from the officer's Settings assignment; add or edit it there if it's missing.

Dealers serviced by this officer

Only dealers this specific officer has sold to — not other officers sharing the same Area/Territory.

Officer confirmation statements

Generate a ready-to-send reconciliation statement for a sales officer, listing their tagged parties' Accounts vs Verification balances with any discrepancy flagged. Copy it and send via WhatsApp, email or SMS — this page does not send messages itself.
